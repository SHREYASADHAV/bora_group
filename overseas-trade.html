SUPPLY CHAIN EXECUTION

Our 5-Stage Export Process

Every overseas cargo follows rigorous traceability and inspection protocols from farm-gate to destination seaports.

STAGE 01

Direct Sourcing

Contract agreements with verified farmer cooperatives, ginning mills, and manufacturing plants ensuring ethical origin.

TRACEABLE BATCHES
STAGE 02

Quality & Lab Testing

Laboratory moisture profiling, laser sorting, aflatoxin analysis, pesticide screen, and weight calibration.

APEDA & SGS TESTING
STAGE 03

Climate Packaging

Custom breathable mesh nets for alliums, food-grade vacuum BOPP liners for grains, and heavy-duty 7-ply cartons.

MOISTURE BARRIERS
STAGE 04

Port Clearance

Customs processing, phytosanitary clearance certification, and container stuffing at JNPT Nhava Sheva / Mumbai ports.

DGFT & CUSTOMS BROKERS
STAGE 05

Global Delivery

Vessel dispatch with complete shipping documents (BL, Phytosanitary, Certificate of Origin, Insurance) to destination ports.
