Industries Covered

Established in 1947 as Bora Brothers and now known as BORA GROUP, the organization is a professionally managed family enterprise with diversified operations across Real Estate, Overseas Trade, Garments & Textiles, Industrial Supplies, and Hospitality & Services. Guided by integrity, operational excellence, and long-term vision, the Group has built a reputation for quality, reliability, and sustainable growth while continuing to create lasting value across every sector it serves.

Legacy Timeline

1947
Company Foundation

Bora Brothers established, entering agricultural commodities trading and regional wholesale distribution.

1975
Overseas Export Expansion

Launched the Overseas Division, exporting premium Indian grains, spices, and oilseeds globally.

1989
Garments & Textiles Entry

Diversified into retail garments and designer apparel, opening Bora Collection and Bora Fashion stores.

2000
Real Estate Launch (Bora Realty)

Established the luxury real estate vertical, developing premier residential complexes and commercial centers.

2014
Hospitality Entry

Launched Hospitality & Services vertical, managing premium business hotels and event properties.

2026+
Digital & Green Future

Integrating solar power grids in construction, smart systems in imports, and pursuing strict ESG criteria.
