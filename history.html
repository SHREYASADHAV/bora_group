Origin Foundations

Bora Brothers Founded

Our legacy begins in 1947 with the founding of "Bora Brothers" in Kopargaon, Maharashtra. Our founders established a foundational presence rooted in transparent trade and commercial execution integrity, dealing with wholesale commodities and agricultural trade.

"Our guiding principles since 1947: trust, fairness, and mutual growth."
Luxury Developments

Real Estate Division Launch

We diversified our business operations into luxury construction and building projects. Focusing on quality developments, architectural values, and structural stability, the Real Estate Division established BORA GROUP as a trusted developer in the region.

"Crafting structural integrity and architectural values."
Logistics & Supplies

Industrial Supplies Expansion

We launched our Industrial Supplies division, distributing high-volume mechanical components, materials, and machinery supplies. The division constructed regional warehousing infrastructure and logistics distribution networks to serve emerging regional businesses.

"Enabling large-scale enterprise logistics infrastructure."
Retail Fashion

Bora Collection Store Launch

BORA GROUP entered the retail fashion and garment segment with the launch of the "Bora Collection" store network, pioneering high-quality apparel designs and establishing a trusted regional consumer retail presence.

"Bringing premium style and retail trust directly to our community."
Global Operations

Overseas Trade Division

We expanded our scope to international markets by establishing the Overseas Trade division, managing complex import-export logistics and commodity trading operations across global destinations.

"Connecting regional commercial activities with international trade flows."
Boutique Hospitality

Hotel Divya Palace Launch

BORA GROUP entered the premium hospitality segment with the launch of "Hotel Divya Palace" in Kopargaon, introducing luxury accommodations, fine banquet spaces, and top-tier guest services.

"Redefining hospitality with elegant design and warm personalized service."
Industrial Apparel

Bora Fashion

We launched "Bora Fashion" as a state-of-the-art apparel production facility, manufacturing high-volume finished garments for domestic fashion retail giants and global export markets.

"Fusing modern manufacturing precision with fashion industry demands."
