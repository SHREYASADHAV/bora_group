EXPLORE HOSPITALITY

A Sanctuary of Comfort in the Heart of Kopargaon

Located in the heart of Kopargaon, Hotel Divya Palace offers comfortable accommodation, modern amenities, and convenient access for business travelers, tourists, and family gatherings. Situated opposite the bus stand, the hotel is known for its accessible location and hospitality services.

Hotel Divya Palace combines comfort, convenience, and hospitality to create memorable experiences for every guest. Whether you're visiting for business, leisure, family celebrations, or special events, our commitment to exceptional service ensures a stay defined by comfort and care.
