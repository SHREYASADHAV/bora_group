About BORA GROUP

Enterprise Profile

Diversification Driven by Integrity & Purpose

Bora Group traces its history to 1947 when it opened operations under the brand "Bora Brothers". For over three quarters of a century, the group has adapted to evolving market demands while upholding strict professional ethics.

Today, our multi-sector operations leverage deep execution knowledge to offer stable performance in Real Estate, Overseas Trade, Garments & Textiles, Industrial Supplies, and Hospitality & Services. Guided by strong internal governance standards, we align modern operations with a time-tested business philosophy.
